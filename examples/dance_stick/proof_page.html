What to judge

  • Car: the whole body drops on every beat and snaps sideways with the drop, then eases back.
  • Car: the heads never tilt or turn inside a beat; faces and the driver's head turn change only where a phrase starts (bars 3 and 5).
  • Car, bars 5 to 8: the passenger's raised arm rides her body with no lag, and her hand stays inside the windshield.
  • Standing: both feet stay on their marks through bob, sway and arm-pump; in step-touch a foot leaves the floor only between its contacts.
  • Standing: the moves blend over one beat at each change; the figure is taller than the Stick room's own standing poses (the skeleton's torso).
