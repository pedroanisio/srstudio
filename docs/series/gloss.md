## The world

The Plaza is a pastel checkerboard that runs to the horizon under a lavender sky, with toy blocks floating
over it. It has an Activity Room, which is white panels and an observation window, and a Waiting Room with a
NOW SERVING display that never reaches you. Nobody remembers arriving, and nobody leaves.

Sunny hosts it. Sunny is a floating, glossy sun-yellow orb with a pink ring and a screen face. It speaks in
the calm, pleasant voice of a facility announcement and is never sorry. The Joy Points board scores every
small act a guest makes, like an afterlife ledger with a customer-service manner.

## The format

- Every reel opens with Sunny asking the hook question, as a card and as the first line.
- Each reel escalates in threes and holds at least 1.2 s before the punchline.
- Each reel buttons on a typed Plaza Notice.
- The punchline is always the system being pleasant about something unbearable from adult life: onboarding,
  scoring, trust exercises, surveys, queues, wellness checks, terms of service.
- Callbacks run across the series: the painted door, the free trial, Gus's key, the points, "premium".
- One composed bed is shared by the series: "Plaza Theme (On Loop)", toy piano and glockenspiel lobby music at
  120 BPM, a little too happy. It is ducked under every line and drops further through every pause between lines,
  so the hold before a punchline is near silence.
