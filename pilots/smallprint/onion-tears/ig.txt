Why do onions make you cry?

Small Print #3. Cutting breaks the onion's cells and makes a gas that reaches your eyes (Imai et al., Nature, 2002). Chilling it first slows that down (UW-Madison, 2009), and a sharp knife throws fewer droplets (Wu et al., PNAS, 2025).
