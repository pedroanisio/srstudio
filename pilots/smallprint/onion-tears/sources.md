## Not on screen, used for the script

- The gas's name is syn-propanethial-S-oxide, written (Z)-propanethial S-oxide in Silvaroli et al., ACS Chemical
  Biology 12(9):2296 (2017), PMC5693239. The brief allowed the name only if it read as a stamp. It does not, so it is
  left out.
- Cutting the root end last is extension advice (NDSU FN1794, 2016) with no measurement behind it. It is left out
  because no measured source backs it. Running water and a fan are left out for the same reason.

## Safety and health

Nothing here is health advice: the tips are about how to cut an onion. Rafa holds the knife on the board, blade down
and away from his hand, and the script says nothing about rubbing or rinsing eyes.
