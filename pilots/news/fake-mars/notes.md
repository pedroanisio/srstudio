## What is claimed, and what is comedy

- "The crew faced isolation, limited resources and delayed messages." is NASA's list of stressors.
- "NASA seeks volunteers for the next one." is NASA's July headline. It does not say applications are
  open today, because NASA's page gives no window.
- "You must pass a psychological assessment." is NASA's requirement. The ticker and lower-third keep it with
  "physical".
- The rent, the landlord, the application and its result are invented. The chat names no agency.
