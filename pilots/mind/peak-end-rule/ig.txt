Five stars. For a mint.

The peak-end rule: we often judge an experience by its worst moment and its last, and how long it lasted barely counts. In one study, 22 of 32 male students chose to repeat the longer of two cold-water trials, the one that ended slightly warmer. Cold water isn't dinner, and for long holidays the evidence is much weaker, so treat it as a thing to try.
Source: Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber & Redelmeier (1993), Psychological Science 4(6).
