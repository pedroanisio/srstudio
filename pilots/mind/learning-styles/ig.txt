The meshing hypothesis: the idea that you learn more when teaching matches your style. Pashler et al. found "no adequate evidence base" for building it into general teaching. Not disproved: a 2024 meta-analysis found a small benefit (g = 0.31), "too small and too infrequent" for widespread adoption.
Source: Pashler et al. (2008); Clinton-Lisell & Litzinger (2024); Knoll et al. (2017); Dunlosky et al. (2013); 89.1% is among educators surveyed, Newton & Salvi (2020).
