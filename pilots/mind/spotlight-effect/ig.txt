The spotlight effect: we expect others to notice us more than they do. In one study, students in an embarrassing T-shirt guessed 46% of the room could say who was on it. 23% could.
Source: Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 78(2).
