One thing in this room is changing. Did you catch it?

Watch it once, then watch it again knowing. Large, slow changes can go unnoticed, and in one study a stranger was swapped mid-chat and only half noticed.
Sources: Simons, Franconeri & Reimer (2000), Perception 29(10); Simons & Levin (1998), Psychonomic Bulletin & Review 5(4).

Voices: ElevenLabs premade voices Bella and Alice. Original characters, drawn in code.
