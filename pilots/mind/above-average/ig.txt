Are you an above-average driver?

There's a Name for That #3. The better-than-average effect: in a 1981 study, 93% of US students and 69% of Swedish students rated themselves more skillful than the median driver. A 2023 replication with American adults found 91%, and 78% on adjusted materials. The hospital drivers are Preston & Harris, 1965, as Svenson reports them. Small samples and self-reports; the tree is ours.
Source: Svenson (1981), Acta Psychologica
