The planning fallacy: when people say a time, they mostly talk about the plan and rarely about the past. Thesis students phoned near the end said about 34 days from that call and took about 56 (37 surveyed; averages from the 33 who had finished). When students pictured how a job could go as usual, the optimism went away. They did not finish any sooner.
Source: Buehler, Griffin & Ross (1994), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 67(3).
