Could you actually explain a zipper?

There's a Name for That #4. The illusion of explanatory depth: in Rozenblit & Keil's first study, nearly all of 16 Yale graduate students rated their understanding of everyday devices lower after trying to explain them. Small university samples, about how things work; the drop was absent or small for procedures, stories and facts. The scores on the board are ours.
Source: Rozenblit & Keil (2002), Cognitive Science
