2. Haha means you didn't laugh, and "Ha." with a period means they know.
3. The one nobody admits: everyone has it on mute, so it's just you in there, alone, typing haha.

Lint". He looks down at the cardigan he is wearing, a strand of it hanging out of his mouth, presses the side button,
and looks at you in the dark of the screen.

chat to be left on read.
