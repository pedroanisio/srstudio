Where would you come up if you dug straight through the Earth?

Maps #3. For about 80% of people, in the sea: 79.8% on GHS-POP 2025 and 80.0% on WorldPop 2025, opposite Natural Earth's land. Only 13% of the land has land opposite. A city here is one coordinate, not its whole footprint, and Beijing is not all of China: Chengdu lands in the sea and Xi'an in Chile.
Source: GHS-POP 2025, antipodes computed against Natural Earth 10m land.
