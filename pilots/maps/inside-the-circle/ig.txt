Why does one circle hold 3.95 billion here and 44 million there?

Around Mong Khet the same circle holds nearly half of everyone alive. The centre in Australia, Adelaide, was chosen so the circle holds the country and largely misses Java; about 19 million of the 44 live outside Australia.
Source: GHS-POP 2025, circle of 3,300 km around Mong Khet, 48.3% of the grid. Lambert equal-area.
